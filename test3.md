## Class 4 – Term 1 – ICSE Board Pattern - **Science Question Paper**

 **Maximum Marks: 80** | **Time Allowed: 1 Hour 45 Mins**

**General Instructions:**
This paper is divided into eight sections, Section A to Section H. All questions are compulsory. Marks allotted to each section are indicated against it. Answers should be written in simple and clear language.

### Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (10 Marks)
**Tick the correct answer for each of the following.** _(10 x 1 = 10)_

**1.** Which of the following parts of a plant remains underground? **a. Root, b. Shoot, c. Branch, d. Bud** **2.** The process in which a solid changes into a liquid on heating is called **a. Evaporation, b. Melting, c. Condensation, d. Freezing** **3.** Which of these plants lives for many years? **a. Mint, b. Banyan, c. Mehndi, d. Bottle gourd** **4.** Everything around us is known as **a. liquid, b. solid, c. matter, d. gas** **5.** In germination, what grows into a plant? **a. leaf, b. flower, c. seed, d. branch** **6.** Which of the following is called the universal solvent? **a. Oil, b. Milk, c. Water, d. Juice** **7.** Which of the following plants needs support to climb? **a. Neem, b. Cotton, c. Pea, d. Rose** **8.** Which of the following can flow easily? **a. Table, b. Chair, c. Water, d. Book** **9.** Which part of a plant has lamina? **a. Flower, b. Fruit, c. Trunk, d. Leaf** **10.** An object floats in water if it displaces **a. less water than its own weight, b. more water than its own weight, c. equal water to its own weight, d. no water at all**

### Section B: Fill in the Blanks (10 Marks)
**Fill in the blanks with the correct word.** _(10 x 1 = 10)_

**1.** The roots bind the plants to the soil and prevent soil ____. **2.** The process in which a liquid changes into vapour on heating is called ____. **3.** ____ is an example of a herb, while Gulmohar is an example of a tree. **4.** Matter is anything that has ____ and occupies ____. **5.** The seed leaves inside a seed are called ____, and the baby plant inside the seed is called the ____. **6.** Substances that dissolve in a solvent are called ____, and those that do not dissolve are called ____. **7.** Cinnamon is the ____ of a plant, and clove is the ____ of a plant. **8.** A liquid takes the shape of the ____ in which it is kept. **9.** The green-coloured pigment in leaves that traps sunlight is called ____. **10.** An object sinks in water if it displaces ____ water than its own weight.

### Section C: True or False (10 Marks)
**State whether the following statements are True or False. Correct the false statements.**  _(10 x 1 = 10)_

**1.** Roots grow towards sunlight. **2.** Herbs live for many years. **3.** Freezing is the reverse of melting. **4.** Solids can flow easily. **5.** Mango and litchi have many seeds. **6.** Cooking food in clay pots is unhealthy. **7.** Oil is soluble in water. **8.** A seed needs warmth, water and air to germinate. **9.** Bigger objects always sink and smaller objects always float in water. **10.** Nuts are seeds of the plant that we eat.

### Section D: Match the Following (5 Marks)
**Match the items in Column A with the correct items in Column B.**  _(5 x 1 = 5)_

**1.** Cherry **2.** Sugar cane **3.** Chickpeas **4.** Spinach **5.** Radish
(Column B: Fruit, Stem, Pulses, Leaf, Root — to be matched in the correct order.)

### Section F: Very Short Answer Questions (10 Marks)
**Answer the following questions in one word or one sentence.**  _(10 x 1 = 10)_

**1.** Name the two main parts of a plant. **2.** What is melting? **3.** Name the five types of plants. **4.** What is matter? **5.** What is germination? **6.** What is a solute? **7.** Name the bark of a plant that is used as a spice. **8.** Name the three forms of matter. **9.** What is a taproot? **10.** Who first explained why some things float and some things sink?

### Section G: Short Answer Questions (20 Marks)
**Answer the following questions in two to three sentences.**  _(10 x 2 = 20)_

**1.** What are stomata? Why are they important? **2.** What are trees? Give one example. **3.** Why does a liquid take the shape of its container? **4.** What is condensation? Give an example. **5.** What are cotyledons? **6.** What are creepers? Give one example. **7.** What are soluble and insoluble substances? Give one example of each. **8.** What is photosynthesis? **9.** What are food grains? Name the two types. **10.** When does an object float and when does it sink in water?

### Section H: Long Answer Questions (15 Marks)
**Answer the following questions in detail.**  _(5 x 3 = 15)_

**1.** Differentiate between the types of roots with examples. **2.** Explain the three different forms of matter with examples. **3.** How can you take care of plants at your home? **4.** Explain the structure of a dicot seed. **5.** Can water change its form? Explain with examples.
