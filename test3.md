## Mathematics2 Practice Paper
**Class 5 — ICSE Board Pattern**
**Maximum Marks: 30 Time: 1 Hour**

**General Instructions:** **1.** This paper is divided into four sections — A, B, C and D. **2.** All questions are compulsory. **3.** Show all working clearly wherever required. **4.** Marks for each question are indicated against it.

### SECTION A
*(5 × 1 = 5 Marks)*

**1.** **a.** Add 265758 and 879899. **b.** Convert the improper fraction 16/7 to a mixed fraction. **c.** Find the first five multiples of 9. **d.** Find the HCF of 15 and 30 by listing all the factors. **e.** Write the reciprocal of 2/5.

### SECTION B
*(5 × 2 = 10 Marks)*

**2.** **a.** Subtract 87654 from 876543. **b.** Compare the fractions 2/5 and 5/8 by cross-multiplication. **c.** Find the prime factors of 84. **d.** Add 3/5 and 7/10. **e.** Find the LCM of 24 and 36 by the prime factorization method.

### SECTION C
*(3 × 3 = 9 Marks)*

**3.** **a.** Multiply 8/9 and 3/4, writing the product in its lowest term. **b.** Find the HCF of 36, 48 and 96 by listing all the factors. **c.** Divide 2 4/5 by 2 1/3.

### SECTION D
*(1 × 6 = 6 Marks)*

**4.** **a.** A merchant had 278534 sacks of potatoes. He sold 143748 sacks in the first week and 25637 sacks in the second week. How many sacks did he sell in all in these two weeks, and how many sacks were left? **b.** A carton had 20 1/2 kg of rice. If 4 1/4 kg of rice was left, how much rice was sold?
