## Mathematics2 Practice Paper
**Class 5 — ICSE Board Pattern**

**Maximum Marks: 40** | **Time Allowed: 1 hour**

**General Instructions:**
1. This paper is divided into four sections — A, B, C and D.
2. All questions are compulsory.
3. Show all working clearly wherever required.
4. Marks for each question are indicated against it.
5. Write answers in the space provided, keeping your work neat and legible.

### SECTION A *(10 × 1 = 10 marks)*

**1.** **a.** Add 723408 and 362897. **b.** Subtract 87654 from 876543. **c.** Convert the improper fraction 16/7 to a mixed fraction. **d.** Reduce 18/27 to its lowest term. **e.** Write the reciprocal of 2/5. **f.** Find the first five multiples of 9. **g.** List all the factors of 18. **h.** Find the HCF of 15 and 30. **i.** What should be added to 17526 to get 30000? **j.** Express 12 as the sum of two prime numbers.

### SECTION B *(5 × 2 = 10 marks)*

**2.** **a.** Compare the fractions 2/5 and 5/8 by cross-multiplication. **b.** Find the LCM of 24 and 36 by the prime factorization method. **c.** Estimate the sum of 128 and 231 to the nearest ten. **d.** Add 2/9 and 4/9. **e.** Find the prime factors of 42.

### SECTION C *(4 × 3 = 12 marks)*

**3.** **a.** Add 90534281, 173462 and 4989537. **b.** Subtract 1 1/4 from 2 3/8. **c.** Find the HCF of 36, 48 and 96 by listing all the factors. **d.** Multiply 8/9 and 3/4, writing the product in its lowest term.

### SECTION D *(2 × 4 = 8 marks)*

**4.** **a.** A merchant had 278534 sacks of potatoes. He sold 143748 sacks in the first week and 25637 sacks in the second week. How many sacks did he sell in all in these two weeks, and how many sacks were left? **b.** Amina bought 3/8 m of red ribbon and 1/2 m of green ribbon; find the total length of ribbon that she bought. Also, a store had 2/3 dozen eggs, of which 1/6 dozen were used to bake a cake; find what fraction of a dozen eggs were left.
