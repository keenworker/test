## ENGLISH GRAMMAR

### A. Verbs – Choosing the Correct Form

**1** Which verb form is correct: "They ___ playing cricket in the afternoon." **play**/**played**/**playing** **2** Which verb form is correct: "The earth ___ around the sun." **revolve**/**revolves**/**revolving** **3** Which verb form is correct: "The boy ___ when his mother called him." **slept**/**was sleeping**/**sleeps** **4** Which verb form is correct: "We will ___ the trophy tomorrow." **lift**/**lifted**/**lifting** **5** Which verb form is correct: "She ___ to Delhi yesterday." **go**/**goes**/**went**
