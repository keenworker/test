### PART 1: ENGLISH GRAMMAR

**Section A: Degrees of Comparison**

A.1 Fill in the blanks:
1. My mother is _______ (pretty) of all.
2. This painting is _______ (colourful) than the first one.
3. Tom is _______ (active) boy in the class.
4. Riha is _______ (beautiful) as her mother.
5. Monty is _______ (fast) runner.
6. The bear is _______ than the lemon. (sweet)
7. Jupiter is _______ planet in the solar system. (large)
8. Shruti is _______ than Vikram. (fit)
9. Usain Bolt is called _______ man on Earth. (fast)
10. Isn't that _______ dog you've ever met? (friendly)

A.2 Underline the adjectives and name their degree of comparison:
1. The text is more important than the concert.
2. Benjy is quite a loyal dog.
3. It is a sharp knife.
4. Tanisha is the youngest in her family.
5. This book is as thick as that one.
