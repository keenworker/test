#### TERM 1 EXAMINATION

**Maximum Marks: 40** | **Time Allowed: 1 Hour**

### SECTION A — Fill in the Blanks (1 mark each) [10 Marks]

**a.** The Constitution of India is the ......... law of the country. **b.** Iron ploughs, called ......... were more efficient than the ancient wooden ploughs. **c.** ......... means that India is an independent country with no interference from other countries in its internal and external matters. **d.** Nails and ......... helped in fixing iron plates. **e.** The Constitution of India came into effect on ......... **f.** The ......... was used for grinding grains to make flour. **g.** All men and women are ......... under the law of the country. **h.** 'Smithing' is the hammering of ......... to give it a required shape. **i.** ......... duties are meant to be performed by the citizens. **j.** Rye and ......... were the two new crops introduced during this age.

### SECTION B — State whether the following statements are True or False (1 mark each) [5 Marks]

**a.** Bronze Age began after the Iron Age. **b.** Dr BR Ambedkar was the chairman of the drafting committee. **c.** Ards helped in fixing iron plates. **d.** There were 299 members in the Constituent Assembly who prepared the draft of the Constitution of India. **e.** People started living in small communities towards the end of the Iron Age.
