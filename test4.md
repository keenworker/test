## COMPUTER - CLASS 5 EXAMINATION - St. Paul's

**Maximum Marks: 40**
**Time Allowed: 1 Hour**

### **SECTION A — MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS (1 × 6 = 6 Marks)**

**Tick (✓) the correct option.**

**1.** The second generation of computers saw the replacement of vacuum tubes with (a) Transistors (b) Integrated circuits (c) Magnetic core memory. **2.** The development of microprocessors happened in which computer generation? (a) Third-generation (b) Fourth-generation (c) Fifth-generation. **3.** Which of the following is the first calculating device? (a) Abacus (b) Pascaline Calculator (c) Difference Engine. **4.** Who is called the Father of the Computer? (a) Charles Babbage (b) Steve Jobs (c) Blaise Pascal. **5.** Who developed the Analytical Engine? (a) Blaise Pascal (b) Charles Babbage (c) Gottfried Wilhelm Von Leibniz. **6.** Type of computer used for weather forecasting: (a) Supercomputer (b) Minicomputer (c) Mainframe computer.

### **SECTION B — TRUE OR FALSE (1 × 4 = 4 Marks)**

**Write T for True and F for False.**

**1.** The first generation of computers used transistors for processing. **2.** Fifth-generation computers primarily focused on artificial intelligence. **3.** One of the limitations of computers is their inability to understand information without instructions from a programmer. **4.** The evolution of computer generations has led to significant advancements in terms of size, processing power, and efficiency.

### **SECTION C — SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (2 × 4 = 8 Marks)**

**Answer the following questions.**

**1.** What did Blaise Pascal invent? What was the purpose of the invention? **2.** Who is known as the Father of the Computer? Why? **3.** What are the different characteristics of a computer? **4.** Explain the motive of the fifth-generation computers.

### **SECTION D — SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (3 × 4 = 12 Marks)**

**Answer the following questions.**

**1.** Describe the main features of the first-generation computers. **2.** Describe the main features of the third-generation computers. **3.** Explain any three types of computers with examples. **4.** Explain any three limitations of computers.

### **SECTION E — LONG ANSWER / APPLICATION-BASED QUESTIONS (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)**

**Answer the following questions.**

**1.** Aniket is working for the National Weather Forecasting agency. Which type of computer is he using to forecast the weather conditions? Explain the features of this type of computer and give two examples. **2.** Trace the evolution of computers by describing at least four early calculating devices or machines in the correct order, along with their inventors and the calculations they could perform.
