# Question Paper
## Class 5 | Subject: English Language & Literature
**Chapter: Arachne — The Weaver** | **Total Marks: 30** | **Time: 1 Hour 30 Minutes**

*General Instructions: Read all questions carefully before answering. All sections are compulsory. Write neatly and clearly.*

## Section A — Multiple Choice Questions
### (Choose the correct option.) [1 Mark Each | Total: 4 Marks]

**1.** What was Arachne famous for in Greece? **A) Painting**, **B) Singing**, **C) Weaving**, **D) Dancing** **2.** How did Athena disguise herself when she visited Arachne? **A) As a young girl**, **B) As an old woman**, **C) As a merchant**, **D) As a goddess** **3.** What did Arachne depict in her tapestry during the contest? **A) The glory of the gods**, **B) The beauty of nature**, **C) The gods as angry and foolish**, **D) The sea and the sky** **4.** What happened to Arachne at the end of the story? **A) She won the contest and became famous**, **B) She was forgiven by Athena**, **C) She was transformed into a spider**, **D) She stopped weaving forever**

## Section B — Fill in the Blanks
### (Fill in each blank with the correct word.) [½ Mark Each | Total: 2 Marks]

**1.** The cloth Arachne wove was as soft as ___________. **2.** Athena was the great goddess of wisdom and ___________. **3.** Arachne's body shrank to the size of a tiny ___________. **4.** Hundreds gathered to watch the two weave, waiting with ___________ breath.

## Section C — Match Column A with Column B
### (Match each item in Column A correctly with Column B.) [½ Mark Each | Total: 2 Marks]

| Column A | Column B |
|----------|----------|
| Loom | a tool used to spin thread or yarn |
| Spindle | a small tool that moves back and forth on a loom |
| Shuttle | a machine used for weaving cloth |
| Tapestry | a large colourful cloth with pictures woven into it |

## Section D — True or False
### (Write T for True and F for False. Correct the statement if it is False.) [½ Mark Each | Total: 2 Marks]

**1.** Athena disguised herself as a young girl to visit Arachne. **2.** Arachne's tapestry showed the glory and greatness of the gods. **3.** Arachne agreed to the weaving contest with Athena. **4.** Athena took pity on Arachne and gave her a new form so she could continue weaving.

## Section E — Write the Meaning of the Words
### (Write the meaning of the following words in simple language.) [1 Mark Each | Total: 2 Marks]

**1.** Boastful **2.** Depicted

## Section F — Make Sentences
### (Use the following words in sentences of your own.) [1 Mark Each | Total: 2 Marks]

**1.** Tapestry **2.** Stern

## Section G — Who Said to Whom?
### (Identify who said the following lines and to whom they were said.) [1 Mark Each | Total: 3 Marks]

**1.** "I taught myself how to weave — the result of my hard work is that no one can weave better than me — not even Athena, who invented weaving!" **2.** "Old age brings wisdom, and that should be welcomed. Your gift is great but it is just that — a gift, given to you by Athena. If I were you, I would thank her." **3.** "We will have a contest of weaving, and if your work is best, then I will weave no more."

## Section H — Sequencing Events
### (Arrange the following events in the correct order as they happened in the story by writing 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 against each.) [2 Marks | Total: 2 Marks]

**a.** Arachne and Athena both sat down and began weaving at their looms. **b.** Arachne became famous for her weaving and grew very boastful. **c.** Athena transformed Arachne into the world's first spider. **d.** Athena disguised herself as an old woman and knocked on Arachne's door. **e.** All who watched the contest declared Athena the winner.

## Section I — Short Answer Questions
### (Answer each question in 2–3 sentences.) [2 Marks Each | Total: 4 Marks]

**1.** Why did Athena visit Arachne in disguise, and what advice did she give her? **2.** Why did Athena win the weaving contest? How was her tapestry different from Arachne's?

## Section J — Reference to Context
### (Read the extract and answer the questions that follow.) [1 Mark Each | Total: 3 Marks]

*"Oh, how can I live," she cried, "now that I must never use the loom or spindle or shuttle again?"*

**a.** Who is speaking in this line and to whom? **b.** Why is the speaker upset? **c.** What did Athena do after hearing this?

## Section K — Think and Answer
### (Answer each question in 4–5 sentences with your own thoughts.) [2 Marks Each | Total: 4 Marks]

**1.** How does Arachne's attitude change from the beginning to the end of the story? What lesson does her journey teach us? **2.** If you were Arachne, would you have challenged Athena? Why or why not? What would you have woven in your tapestry?
