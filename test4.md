## Class 3 Carmel Sch - SST: Understanding Changes | **Total Marks: 30 | Time: 1 Hour**

### Section A – Multiple Choice Questions (5 × 1 = 5 Marks)

**1.** **a)** Sometimes, seats of students are shuffled and they are made to sit with new — (i) old friends (ii) classmates (iii) teachers **b)** How should we behave with our new classmates? (i) We should misbehave with them (ii) We should not talk to them (iii) We should make them feel comfortable **c)** At birth, a baby's eye is about what per cent of an adult eye? (i) 50 (ii) 75 (iii) 90 **d)** What kind of change is the marriage of a family member? (i) large (ii) small (iii) no change **e)** Which of the following is a change we see in the school? (i) Change of neighbour (ii) Change of food (iii) Change of teacher

### Section B – Fill in the Blanks (5 × 1 = 5 Marks)

**2.** **a)** Kolkata was earlier known as ______. **b)** Chennai was earlier known as ______. **c)** Sometimes, schools also hire new support staff or ______. **d)** Babies can see only in ______ colours for a few months. **e)** When new students join a class, we should make them feel ______.

### Section C – True or False (5 × 1 = 5 Marks)

**3.** **a)** Many times, people have to move to a new city because of their job. **b)** When we make new friends, we should not care about old friends. **c)** We should only sit with our best friends in class. **d)** Some changes take place on a large scale while some take place on a small scale. **e)** Schools never change their Principal.

### Section D – Match the Following (5 × 1 = 5 Marks)

**4.** **a)** school **b)** Principal **c)** cleaning staff **d)** Chennai **e)** small change — matches with — (i) change of colour preference (ii) Madras (iii) head of the school (iv) support staff (v) uniform

### Section E – Short Answer Questions (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)

**5.** **a)** Why is it important to make new friends in class? **b)** What are large-scale changes? Give two examples. **c)** What happens when people move to a new city? **d)** How should we help new students who join our class? **e)** Mention two changes you notice every year in your school.
